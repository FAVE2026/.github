## Introduction
Project 300 represents a substantial body of work undertaken by our group that represents the culmination of our learning in the
modules undertaken in Years 1, 2 and 3. The module aims to encourage innovation and a wider exploration in their field of study.
The module intent is to provide students with a simulation of a real-world project along with the management issues (of time,
people, resources, and skills) and fixed deadlines that accompany it.
